Frequently asked questions

Short answers to the questions people ask before they start.

Do I need any resume writing experience?

No. Fill in the fields in plain language and the builder handles the layout, spacing and headings. The writing assistance panel also includes example summaries and achievement bullet points you can adapt — but they are labelled as examples, so only use what is genuinely true for you.

Can I edit a template after choosing it?

Yes. Switching templates never deletes your content. You can also change the accent colour at any time from the preview toolbar.

How do I download my resume?

Use the Download PDF button in the builder. If the PDF library cannot be loaded (for example, you are offline), ResumeCraft automatically opens your browser's print dialog instead, where you can choose "Save as PDF".

Is my personal information private?

Yes. Everything you type is stored only in your own browser using localStorage. ResumeCraft does not send your resume anywhere and does not collect personal information. If you clear your browser data, the saved copy is removed — so export a JSON backup if you want a permanent copy.

What happens after I pay?

You'll be redirected to Stripe's secure checkout. Once your payment goes through, you'll be sent back here and your features unlock automatically on this browser. Your Stripe receipt email contains a link to view the order — that link is your proof of purchase if you ever need to unlock on a different device. Click Already purchased? Restore access on the pricing section to enter it.

Can I use this on my phone?

Yes. On smaller screens you can switch between the editor and the preview with the buttons above the workspace, so you are not scrolling past a long form to check your layout.
